## Lessons

- Fixed `num_warps` and increasing `BLOCK` can change `sizePerThread` and scalar vs. vector PTX memory operations.
- Registers/thread also vary with configuration, but register count alone cannot establish the reason for timing differences.
- ~4.14 TB/s is **effective logical bandwidth** with cache reuse, not an observed DRAM rate.
- These findings motivate controlled matrix multiplication and profiler experiments.
